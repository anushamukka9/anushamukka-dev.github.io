I speak about the parts of large systems that only show up in production: keeping ranking systems reliable, fast, and honest under real load, and the access-control infrastructure that decides what is allowed in the first place.

- **[When Your Model Meets Reality: Designing ML Ranking Systems That Survive Billions of Daily Requests](https://odsc.ai/speakers-portfolio/when-your-model-meets-reality-designing-ml-ranking-systems-that-survive-billions-of-daily-requests/)** - ODSC AI West (virtual talk). On inference pipelines under strict latency budgets, feature systems that stay reliable when upstream data degrades, graceful degradation, and the feedback loop between production performance and retraining.
- **[Policy Decision Engines: The Infrastructure Behind Enterprise Access Control](https://alldayai.com/)** - All Day AI (virtual talk, SECURE track). How to design a policy decision engine: where the decision point lives, how to keep evaluations fast enough for the request path, and how to write policies teams can actually maintain, plus the production lessons: policy versioning, auditability for compliance, fail-safe defaults, and what breaks when policy becomes a distributed systems problem.
