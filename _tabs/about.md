I'm Anusha Mukka, a software architect specializing in AI-powered infrastructure, distributed systems, and security architecture.

Most of my career has been spent on infrastructure where the stakes are real: platforms that have to scale, systems that have to stay up, and security for data that genuinely matters. I spent several years working on Minnesota's statewide eCharging platform, designing workflows, integrations, and shared infrastructure for the state's electronic criminal-charging pipeline. I've also built across consumer tech and healthcare.

The through-line has always been the same: taking an ambiguous, high-stakes problem and turning it into an architecture that holds.

This is where I write about that work and the ideas behind it — essays and technical notes on the systems I build and the decisions behind them.
